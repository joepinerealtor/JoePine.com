JoePine.com

App type: Static multi-page web app/site

Repo snapshot date: 2026-04-17

What It Is

JoePine.com is a branded, client-facing real-estate website for Joe Pine covering Rhode Island and Massachusetts. It combines educational guides, calculators, search/listing experiences, and process explainers so clients can understand the numbers and the next step.

Who It's For

Primary persona: Rhode Island and Massachusetts home buyers and sellers, especially first-time buyers who want plain-language education, planning tools, and direct access to Joe Pine.

What It Does

  • Publishes a 28-slide first-time buyer workbook covering credit, cash to close, assistance, contingencies, and offer-to-closing steps.
  • Provides a buyer hub with the journey tracker, monthly payment calculator, lender list, inspector list, and attorney options.
  • Provides a seller hub with the seller journey, seller proceeds calculator, and closing-attorney options.
  • Wraps Keller Williams home search with a quick payment-based budget estimator and full-search links.
  • Renders featured listings from repo-managed listing data, including photos, status notes, open-house info, and tour links.
  • Exports branded calculator worksheets/PDFs; the seller proceeds tool also remembers inputs locally.

How It Works

  • Front end: static HTML/CSS/JS pages under folders such as buyers/, sellers/, journey/, Monthly Payment/, Seller Net/, search/, and listings/, with shared chrome in site-frame.css and site-frame.js.
  • Composition: buyer and seller hubs embed sub-pages in iframes and resize them with postMessage; redirect/file-preview helpers support local file: viewing.
  • Client-side data: content and state live in browser scripts such as script.js, journey.js, listings-data.js, and calculator code inside page HTML; seller proceeds persistence uses cookies plus localStorage.
  • External services: Leaflet plus CARTO/OpenStreetMap tiles, Keller Williams search embeds, Mortgage News Daily rates via CodeTabs proxy, and Firebase Auth/Firestore in Monthly Payment/index.html for anonymous auth and lead capture.
  • Repo-owned backend/build system: Not found in repo.

How To Run

  • Install/build/start command: Not found in repo.
  • Open index.html in a browser for the minimal local preview; route wrappers and site-frame.js include explicit file: handling.
  • Optional inference from repo structure: serve the folder as static files if you want cleaner route URLs and iframe behavior.
